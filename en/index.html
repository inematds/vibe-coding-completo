What Is Vibe Coding: Complete Mastery

🗣️

You describe, the AI builds

Instead of learning the mechanics of each node or line of code, you describe the desired result. AI selects tools, configures them, and debugs. You’re the manager; AI is the developer.

🧭

Six entry points

Start with theory (T1), technical skills (T2), prompts (T3), skills and agents (T4), projects (T5), or production (T6). The learning paths are independent but cross-reference one another.

🚀

From concept to production

You’ll leave understanding every term, mastering the technique, with a ready-to-use library of prompts and skills, and building real agents and apps that run on their own in the cloud—with security and monetization.

🎯 Who it’s for

From a complete beginner (starts in T1) to someone who already builds and wants to master techniques and agents (starts in T2/T4). Prerequisites: a computer, free accounts for the tools, and a willingness to iterate. No prior programming knowledge required — in foundational modules, each technical term is defined at first mention.
